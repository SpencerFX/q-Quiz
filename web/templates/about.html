About q-Quiz

q-Quiz is a practice platform for learning the q/kdb+ language, built around a live q server. Multiple-choice questions and codepad exercises are graded directly against real q evaluation, not pattern matching.

Fundamentals

Multiple Choice / Multiple Choice - Syntax
Two independently-tracked question banks covering q concepts and syntax.
qIdioms
Idiom-style exercises organized by topic (arithmetic, strings, joins, and more), graded via the codepad.
Fundamentals
Codepad exercises on operator overloads and qsql, split into easy/medium/hard tiers.

Challenges

AquaQ Challenges
Codepad problems referencing the diChallenges question set.
HackerRank Problems
Algorithms and data structure problems, graded against multiple test cases.
leetcode
A selection of classic LeetCode problems reimplemented for the codepad.
quantRank
Probability and quant-interview style problems.
